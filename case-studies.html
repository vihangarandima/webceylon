Dossier 02 • IoT & Smart Grid Mesh

ChargeUp EV Ceylon — Island-Wide EV Routing Platform

National Green Award

The Strategic Problem

Rapid EV adoption in Sri Lanka outpaced station visibility. Drivers faced long charging queues in remote hill country areas without live kW load indicators.

Architectural Solution

Deployed a Go-based microservice mesh consuming OCPP charger telemetry, paired with TimescaleDB time-series storage and sub-85ms client WebSockets for real-time station reservation.

140+ Grid Nodes Synced
99.98% Operational Uptime
